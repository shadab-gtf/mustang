# 1967 Mustang Fastback reference board

The model follows Ford's 1967 2+2 Fastback dimensions, the finished car's appearance in *The Fast and the Furious: Tokyo Drift*, and the RB26 engine fitted to the production's close-up car. *Hot Rod* tested the RB26 car alongside two V8 stunt cars in 2006. Engine builder Sean Morris reports that the Petersen identifies the surviving Hero #1 as a 1968 Fastback. The requested 1967 shell and the hero powertrain are a deliberate combination. [Ford press specifications](https://www.1967shelbyresearch.com/library/docs/files/1966-09-16%20Ford%20Press%20Release%20-%201967%20Mustang.pdf) · [2006 production-car track test](https://www.hotrod.com/news/hdrp-0607-movie-car-builds) · [Sean Morris's build and museum account](https://www.gtrusablog.com/2020/03/tokyo-drift-rb26-powered-mustang-video.html)

## Dimensions and powertrain

| Ford 1967 2+2 Fastback specification | Inches | Meters |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Overall length | 183.6 | 4.663 |
| Overall body width | 70.9 | 1.801 |
| Factory height | 51.8 | 1.316 |
| Wheelbase | 108.0 | 2.7432 |
| Factory front and rear track | 58.0 | 1.4732 |

The factory dimensions establish the stock body scale. Wheel offset, tire profile and ride height vary with the film and auction builds. [Ford general specifications table](https://www.1967shelbyresearch.com/library/docs/files/1966-09-16%20Ford%20Press%20Release%20-%201967%20Mustang.pdf)

The production cars tested by Edmunds used 245/35R19 front and 275/35R19 rear Toyo Proxes T1R tires on gray Volk Racing GT-7 wheels. Those sizes yield nominal unloaded radii of 0.327 m and 0.338 m. The production used Global West suspension components and rack-and-pinion steering. [2006 Edmunds road test](https://www.edmunds.com/car-reviews/features/tokyo-drift-test-v8-powered-1967-ford-mustang.html)

Nissan specifies the donor RB26DETT as a 2,568 cc DOHC inline-six with factory twin turbochargers. The film team fitted a single Turbonetics turbo and a rear-wheel-drive Nissan five-speed manual. The period RB26 test measured 340 hp at 7,300 rpm and 264 lb-ft (358 N·m) at 5,950 rpm at the rear wheels. The tested RB26 car used a Ford 9-inch rear end with a spool. Dennis McCarthy also describes the 430-cubic-inch Ford Windsor engines fitted for stunt work. [Nissan Heritage Collection](https://www.nissan-global.com/EN/HERITAGE_COLLECTION/skyline_gt-r_1991.html) · [Sean Morris's build notes](https://www.gtrusablog.com/2010/06/rb26-powered-ford-mustang.html) · [Edmunds RB26 test](https://www.edmunds.com/car-reviews/features/tokyo-drift-test-skyline-powered-1967-ford-mustang.html) · [Dennis McCarthy interview](https://www.edmunds.com/car-reviews/features/behind-the-scenes-of-the-fast-and-the-furious-tokyo-drift.html)

The factory fastback uses rear-quarter ventilation trim, a fixed windshield and fixed rear glass. The movable glazing is in the doors. Ford describes the sweeping roofline, restyled front and rear, interior ventilation and the factory 2+2 cabin in its 1967 material. [Ford illustrated facts booklet](https://www.xr793.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/1967-Ford-Mustang-Facts-Booklet.pdf) · [Ford sales brochure](https://ponynsnake.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/access-1967_Ford_Mustang_brochure_AR_96_212010_18065.pdf)

The exact film paint formula, stripe dimensions, wheel offsets, suspension travel, spring rates, hero-car mass and engine hose routing are unverified. The restoration gallery also records revised running gear and interior trim. Use the photos to set the shape and visible detailing, and treat unseen details and simulation tuning as estimates. [Seller's recorded changes and production-title images](https://bringatrailer.com/listing/1967-ford-mustang-fastback-152/)

## Custom geometry

- Build the body to the factory wheelbase and width. Set fender positions from the wheel centers, and fit the hood, rear deck, sail panels and cabin to the front, side and rear references.
- Build separate hood, trunk, door skins and cards, window guides and door glass. Build the engine bay, cabin and trunk openings around the intended travel of those moving panels.
- Model the lower-valance intercooler, bright grille and bumper surrounds, exposed rear outlet, seven-spoke wheels, staggered tires, individual rear lenses, single-turbo RB26 and cabin trim for the selected appearance.
